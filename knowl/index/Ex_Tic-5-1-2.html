Players have perfect information. This means each player knows what all of his or her own options are, what all of his or her opponent’s options are, and both players know what the outcome of each option is. Additionally, players know that both players have all of this information. Be careful, though, as some games of chance may also be games with perfect information. Players just need to know what all their opponent’s options are. If some information about which strategies are avalable to a player is hidden, then the game does not have perfect information. Many board games have perfect information in that once a player rolls a die, all the players know what options a player has. Many card games do not have perfect information in that if you don’t know what cards your opponent holds, you don’t know what the payoff will be for certain strategies.
