Activity 3.4.3. The \((0.6, 0.4)\) strategy for Player 2.

In the Matching Pennies game, suppose Player 2 plays H 60% of the time and T 40% of the time.
🔗

(a)

What are \(P_2(H)\) and \(P_2(T)\text{?}\)
🔗
🔗

(b)

What do you think Player 1 should do? Does this differ from your ideal mixed strategy in Activity 3.4.2? Explain.
🔗
🔗

(c)

We can use expected value to compute what Player 1 should do in response to Player 2’s 60/40 strategy. First, consider a pure strategy for Player 1. Compute the expected value for Player 1 if she only plays H while Player 2 plays H with probability \(0.6\) and T with probability \(0.4\text{.}\) This expected value is \(E_1(H)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

(d)

Similarly, compute the expected value for Player 1 if she plays only T. Call it \(E_1(T)\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

(e)

Which pure strategy, H or T, has a higher expected value for Player 1? If Player 1 plays this pure strategy, will she do better than your predicted value of the game?
