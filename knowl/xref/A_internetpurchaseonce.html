Activity 4.7.1. A single internet purchase.

Suppose the matrix in Table 4.7.1 represents the situation of purchasing a rare book on the internet where both parties are untraceable. You agree to send the money at the same time that the seller agrees to send the book. Then we can think of cooperating as each of you sending money/ book, and defecting as not sending money/ book. Why might a player cooperate? Why might a player defect?
